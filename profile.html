/ ID Verified

Verify your identity

ID Verified is completely optional — you can use every other part of Music Motel, including booking and being booked, without it. Before we start, here's exactly what happens:

  • What we collect: a photo of a government-issued ID (passport, national ID, or driver's licence) and a short live camera session where you'll be asked to move your head and smile.
  • Why: solely to confirm you're a real person and issue the "ID Verified" badge on your profile. It is never used for anything else.
  • Retention, in this preview: nothing is uploaded or saved anywhere — every frame stays in your browser and is discarded the moment this flow ends or you close it.
  • Retention, in a real launch: raw images/video would be retained only as long as the verification provider's session requires, then deleted — never kept indefinitely, never used to train models, and never sold or shared.
  • Your rights: this is optional, you can stop at any point before finishing, you can retry for free, and you can request deletion of your verification record at any time from the Verification card on your dashboard.
Read the full privacy & biometric data notice

This notice is specific to ID + Liveness verification — it is not part of Music Motel's general Terms, because biometric consent must be its own clear, informed choice, not something buried in a broader agreement.

South Africa (POPIA): Your ID photo and liveness video are "special personal information." We only process it with your explicit consent, for the single purpose of issuing the ID Verified badge, and keep only what's needed for the shortest time necessary. You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of this data at any time, and we'll notify affected users if a breach involving this data ever occurs.

EU / UK (GDPR): Biometric data used for identification is a "special category" under Article 9 — processed here only with your explicit, freely-given, specific, informed, and unambiguous consent, which you can withdraw at any time with no effect on your ability to use the platform. A real launch serving EU users would complete a Data Protection Impact Assessment first and evaluate EU AI Act obligations for biometric identification systems.

United States — Illinois (BIPA): Before any collection, you receive written notice (this screen) that biometric identifiers are being collected, the specific purpose (the ID Verified badge), and the retention period (immediately discarded in this preview). Checking the box below is your written release. We do not sell, lease, trade, or profit from biometric data. A production system would publish a written retention/destruction schedule and destroy data within 3 years of your last interaction, or sooner once its purpose is fulfilled.

California (CPRA): Biometric information used to identify you is "sensitive personal information." You can direct us to limit its use, and request deletion, through the Verification card at any time.

In production, this flow would hand off entirely to a certified identity provider (e.g. AWS Rekognition Face Liveness, Persona, Onfido, Jumio, or iProov) operating under its own security, encryption, and retention policy and a signed data processing agreement restricting its use of your data — Music Motel would store only the verification result (pass/fail, a confidence score, and the provider's session reference), never a long-lived biometric template. Custom biometric anti-spoofing is intentionally not built here; a real launch requires a certified provider and a privacy lawyer's review of this notice, the consent flow, and the retention schedule.
