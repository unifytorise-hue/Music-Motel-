/ Pricing

  • Full profile with gig history

  • List instruments/gear in the donation board

/ Request a quote

Request —

Ask if they're available on your date, and what their estimate is — apart from travel.

They'll confirm availability and reply with a quote — you won't be charged anything by sending this request. If you accept their quote, it becomes a formal proforma invoice. Music Motel takes a 10% booking fee once a quote is accepted and the gig is paid for, which includes payment transaction processing costs — the artist keeps 90%.
