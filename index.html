/ 01 — About

The gap isn't talent. It's connection.

Music Motel is the directory that closes it.

A vocalist in New York doesn't know the sound engineer two boroughs over. A bassist in Midrand can't find the touring band that needs exactly her skill set.

Music Motel closes that gap. Search by instrument, skill, or location, view a real profile, and connect directly — no agency, no guesswork.

Free for every musician and fan. Paid tools for the venues, educators, and businesses that book them.

/ Request a quote

Request —

Ask if they're available on your date, and what their estimate is — apart from travel.

They'll confirm availability and reply with a quote — you won't be charged anything by sending this request. If you accept their quote, it becomes a formal proforma invoice. Music Motel takes a 10% booking fee once a quote is accepted and the gig is paid for, which includes payment transaction processing costs — the artist keeps 90%.
