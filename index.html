/ 01 — About

A universal language deserves a place to gather.

For years, music has brought people from every background together. Music Motel exists to close the gap between everyone who makes that happen.

Music Motel was born from a simple observation: the music industry is full of talented, gifted people who never find each other. A vocalist in New York doesn't know the sound engineer two boroughs over. A bassist in Midrand can't find the touring band that needs exactly her skill set.

Music Motel closes that gap. It's a social platform built specifically for the music world — search by instrument, skill, or location, view a real profile, and unify with the person who can make your next project happen.

Whether the connection is professional or just for fun, the goal stays the same: end the disconnect, keep the passion of gifted individuals inspired, and give the industry a home base.
