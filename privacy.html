Music Motel ("Music Motel," "we," "us," or "our") operates a directory and marketplace connecting musicians, engineers, businesses, and other music-industry professionals ("the Service"). This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, how it's used and shared, and the choices and rights you have.

1. Information we collect

Account information

When you sign up, we collect your email address, a hashed password (or, if you sign in with Google, your name, email, and profile photo from your Google account), and the account type you select (e.g. musician, fan, business, manager/agent).

Profile information

Anything you choose to add to your public profile: your name, bio, instruments/skills, music taste, a photo or avatar, media portfolio (audio, video, or image links), rate card, and release/credit information you import (see "Third-party services" below).

Location information

To power city-based search, we collect a city or coordinate location you provide directly, or that you select via an interactive map or your device's GPS (with your permission).

Identity verification data — sensitive

If you complete our optional ID + liveness verification, we collect an image of your government-issued ID document and a short liveness capture of your face, used solely to confirm you're a real person and to compare against your ID photo. This is biometric and government-ID data, and we treat it as more sensitive than the rest of your profile:

  • It is used only for identity verification and fraud prevention, never for public display or marketing.
  • You can permanently delete your verification data at any time from your profile's verification settings.
  • [DESCRIBE ACTUAL RETENTION PERIOD AND STORAGE LOCATION ONCE FINALIZED — e.g. "deleted automatically after verification completes" or "retained for N days for fraud-review purposes."]

Booking, payment, and transaction information

Details of bookings you make or receive (dates, amounts, messages between the parties), reviews, and — once payments are enabled — payment method and transaction information. [PAYMENT PROCESSOR NAME, ONCE INTEGRATED] processes card details directly; we do not store full card numbers.

Usage and device information

Standard technical data collected automatically: IP address, browser and device type, pages visited, and referral source, collected via our hosting/CDN provider's server logs.

Local storage

We use your browser's local storage (not third-party tracking cookies) to remember things like whether you've already claimed a one-time bonus, or your last-used search filters, so the Service works smoothly across visits.

2. How we use your information

  • To create and operate your account and public profile.
  • To power location- and category-based search across the directory.
  • To verify identity and help prevent fraud and impersonation.
  • To process bookings, payments, and platform fees, and to communicate with you about them.
  • To send account, booking, and activity notifications.
  • To provide customer support and respond to your requests.
  • To maintain the security and integrity of the Service.
  • To comply with legal obligations.

3. Who we share information with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Other users — your public profile is visible to anyone using the directory; booking and message details are visible to the other party in that booking.
  • Service providers who process data on our behalf under contract, currently: Supabase (database, authentication, and file storage), Cloudflare (hosting and content delivery), and Google (sign-in, if you choose that option).
  • Third-party lookups you trigger — city search queries are sent to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim geocoding service; artist credit imports are sent to MusicBrainz/Discogs. Only the search terms you enter are sent, not your account details.
  • [PAYMENT PROCESSOR NAME], once payment processing is enabled, to handle transactions.
  • Law enforcement or regulators, where required by law or to protect the rights, safety, or property of Music Motel or others.
