What we use today

Music Motel does not currently use third-party advertising or analytics cookies. What we do use:

  • Essential local storage — your browser's local storage (not a cookie sent to our servers) remembers things like whether you've already claimed a one-time signup bonus, your last-used search filters, and similar preferences, so the Service works smoothly across visits. This is necessary for the Service to function and isn't used for tracking or advertising.
  • Authentication — signing in sets a session token (via Supabase Auth) so you stay logged in between visits. This is strictly necessary for the Service to work.

What we don't do

We don't use cookies or local storage to track you across other websites, build advertising profiles, or sell your browsing activity to third parties.

Your choices

You can clear your browser's local storage and cookies at any time through your browser settings; doing so will sign you out and reset any locally-remembered preferences.
